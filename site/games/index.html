Levitating Larry

A man named Larry. He levitates. Brown hair, sharp suit, red cape, and absolutely no interest in coming back down.

He has floated through three games so far: the original, where you kept him aloft one tap at a time through about 70 levels; Levitating Larry 2, which took the floating to phones; and Levitating Larry Returns, a retro pixel run through an endless, mysterious dungeon, and the look Larry wears today.
