▲ HUNGERHOLD

Help your people outlive you.

Request playtest access What is it?

EVERY LIFE TEACHES THE NEXT

You wake in a cave with no memory, starving and thirsty. Find water, light a fire before the wolves come, and cook before your food rots. You'll probably die. That's the point: your memories pass to the next generation, and they go further than you did.

Food is money, and it rots

There's no gold. You pay workers in food, feed your people with food, and watch hoarded food spoil. Wealth has to keep moving.

From cave to kingdom

Climb Maslow's pyramid of needs: survive alone, build a cabin, lead a village. Each tier changes how you play, from your own hands to a camp that runs itself.

The danger is us

Curiosity, fire, greed and ambition bring the biggest rewards and the worst disasters. The world responds to what you do, not to a timer.

WHERE IT'S AT

Prototype 0.2.1 is in closed testing with a small group. Rough edges and placeholder art included. Want in? Request playtest access.

  • Survive the cave: hunger, thirst, fire, wolves, rain, and a ceiling that creaks before it falls.
  • Build a camp: hire foragers and hunters with food; a cellar keeps some fresh and the rest spoils.
  • Die and remember: each death returns memories you spend on permanent knowledge.
  • Keyboard, mouse or touch, with progress that saves between sessions.

Coming: generations and heirs with inherited traits, a living world that picks its own moments, a village and beyond, other peoples to trade with or fight. A Steam page is coming later.

Made by people: we're commissioning pixel artists, composers and sound designers to replace the placeholder art and sound, and everyone gets credited in the game. Interested? partners@biosicstudios.com
