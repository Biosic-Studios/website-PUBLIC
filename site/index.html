Out now

Version 2.2.1The ground gives way

  • Ledges drop. Dig under one and it trickles, then falls. A stone pick splits boulders and cracks old ruin walls.
  • Storms bring gales. Weak trees come down and a lean-to can tear away. Lightning leaves craters and floods eat the riverbanks.
  • Still fair. The valley warns you first, and your stone says how it happened.
  • A better first minute. Every new family wakes to its own opening, the cave has secrets, and making things explains itself.
  • Little things. 30 new achievements ("Probably fine": eat the red mushroom), a sky for every homeland, fairer boars, and about forty fixes.

In the works

Version 3.0The same game, and now it's ours

  • A valley with a voice. The Elder tells you off for eating raw meat, and you miss her when she's gone.
  • Incidents. Leave the pit dark and a raccoon robs the hearth store. A goat eats the lean-to you just built.
  • The handover, done properly. Your stone becomes a Hand down card, and the lesson passes to your heir.
  • Know-how. At a boulder or a fire, see what your family can make there and where to find what's missing.
  • Two things by hand, if you like. Strike the spark yourself, or stare a wolf down with a burning brand.
  • Every heir their own person, new looks for the swamp, the snow and the deep, and a camp dog for every family.
  • Streaming mode, so chat can vote on who wakes next.

All in progress, none of it shipped yet. No date, and some of it may change. More on v3.0 →
