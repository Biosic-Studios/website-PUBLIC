Privacy

THE SHORT VERSION

No cookies. No ads. Nothing that follows you around the web. We count visits to this site anonymously, and that's all.

What we count

We use GoatCounter, a small, open-source visit counter that sets no cookies and stores no personal data. For each page view it records which page you opened, which site or link sent you here, your browser and screen size, and your rough location (country), so we can see what people read and where they come from. It can't tell who you are, and it doesn't follow you to other sites.

We also count clicks on our "Play" links to itch.io, so we know if the site helps people find our games.

What we don't do

  • No cookies, and no ID that follows you from site to site.
  • No ads, no ad trackers, no social media pixels.
  • No accounts and no sign-up forms.
  • Our fonts and most images load from this site, not from third parties.

Other sites we link to

Our games are played on itch.io, and our trailers live on YouTube. A trailer only loads YouTube's player (in its privacy-enhanced mode) when you press play. A few game covers load from itch.io's image server. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

If you email us

The contact page only fills in an email for you to send from your own account; nothing is sent from this site. If you write to us, we use your address to reply, and that's it.

Questions about any of this: partners@biosicstudios.com
